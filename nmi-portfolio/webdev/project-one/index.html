Reflection

This project was a challenge due to my previous understanding of HTML and CSS colliding with the structured way of learning the languages. It challenged me to structure my code in a way that is easy to understand and follows the ciriculum. I learned a lot about CSS and how to navigate the grid and flexbox system. I could not figure out how to stack my images onto texts at first, then that was solved and my next problem was the cards. I had 5 cards for the display of my experience in Nigeria but the bottom cards were off-centered. This was extremely frusttrating and I eventually gave in and added another card just for them to be even. For the food page it was relitavely smooth, i used a generic image slider and combined it with a little javascript to make it work. Overall I am proud of my of how it turned out. Lastly, the timeline page had inspiration from a website I made in december. It worked in a sense but when trying to add pictures to help with the descriptions, it failed. I tried time and time again but it never worked out. Eventually I just used no pictures and continued with the clean look. Overall I am very proud of my project and how it turned out, It gave me a learning experience with fundamentals and even uploading files to cyberduck.
